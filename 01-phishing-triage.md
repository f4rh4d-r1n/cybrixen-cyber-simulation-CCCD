# 📧 Mission 1: Phishing triage

> **Score: 10 / 10** 🟢 · 📍 Reception · 🛠️ Cybrixen Mail (shared front-desk inbox) · 🏷️ Email security, Social engineering

## 🎯 The mission
The receptionist forwarded the shared inbox. Every message needed a decision: **report as phishing** or **mark as safe**. Each message was worth equal points, and leaving one undecided scored nothing.

## 🧠 How I approached it
I read every email like an attacker would write it, then checked it against a short mental checklist:

| 🚩 Red flag | Example pattern |
|------------|-----------------|
| ⏰ Artificial urgency | "Expires in 24 hours", "expires tomorrow" |
| 😨 Threat of losing access | Account or password about to be disabled |
| 🎁 Curiosity bait | A shared "salary review" file, unread messages waiting |
| 🔗 Unexpected action requested | Click a link to keep, renew or confirm something |

And the signs of a **genuine** email: it fits normal business routine (fire drill, benefits enrolment, interview schedule), asks you to do nothing risky, and even reminds people to use the Report button.

## ✅ Result
All 8 messages classified correctly: genuine ones kept, phishing ones reported.

## 📚 Lessons
- Urgency plus a link is the classic combination, so treat it as suspicious until proven otherwise.
- Not everything unusual is malicious. Marking real emails as safe matters just as much as catching fakes.
- The decision stays editable until the end of the shift, so a second pass is worth it.
